## What can move forward

CC0 candidates may proceed to a small download/inspection pilot. Keep the actual archive license, original download URL, date, version and SHA-256 with each selected file. Credit is optional under the verified CC0 grants; ESSAIM should still retain creator provenance. The [CC0 deed and legal code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) explain its scope; it does not erase separate trademark or other third-party rights.

For CC BY, verify the exact license version, then record title, creator, source, license URL and modifications. A search snippet displaying CC Attribution is insufficient to approve an inaccessible Sketchfab download.

For MIT tools, preserve copyright and license text if code is copied. Tool licensing does not license Project Zomboid textures or comparison crops. No tool has been installed in this pass.

## Holds that must remain visible

- A07: itch.io states CC0 but CGTrader currently displays a custom license. Use the exact package license/creator clarification to resolve this; do not combine terms from different platforms.
- A19: editing and redistribution require Daddy Dirkie Dirk's written permission.
- A20: PZ modpack/map permissions do not explicitly cover ESSAIM distribution.
- A21: throttlekitty publishes broad credited-use/modification permission. Confirm standalone scope and credited third-party file origins; do not describe the pack as CC0.
- A22: continuing a PZ vehicle mod is different from permission to redistribute models in a separate game. Obtain an explicit grant and identify contributors.
- A23: a free price tag supplies no missing reuse license.
- A24: raw-file redistribution is restricted, which is relevant to a public repository. Clarify acceptable packaging before any selection.

[Project Zomboid's official modding policy](https://projectzomboid.com/blog/modding-policy/) does not grant ESSAIM ownership of original game art. No vanilla Project Zomboid or Fallout game files, extracted map imagery, textures, logos, animations or ripped models are cleared by this register. No paywall, login or download restriction was bypassed. Permissions have not been requested or obtained through this pass; the next-step template is in [PERMISSIONS.md](docs/asset_discovery/PERMISSIONS.md).

## Quaternius license-scope hold (2026-11-12)

The downloaded Universal Animation Library Standard archive contains a `License.txt` stating CC0 1.0, and its [itch.io package page](https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-animation-library) labels the asset CC0. Quaternius' separate [Asset License (QAL v1.0)](https://quaternius.github.io/license.html), updated 2026-08-28, permits commercial products and requires no credit but restricts redistribution of raw assets. The live statements conflict on whether the Standard files can be redistributed in ESSAIM's source repository.

Per the project's license gate, A01 and A02 remain `LICENSE_CONFLICT_HOLD`; do not bundle them until Quaternius clarifies which terms govern these exact Standard archives. The 43-clip GLB was temporarily imported to inspect its 65-bone rig and Godot compatibility, then removed from `assets/`. The archive stays in ignored `.tools/asset-discovery/downloads/`; `assets/licenses/quaternius/universal_animation_library/LICENSE.txt` is retained only as the package's license evidence. No external permission request was sent.
