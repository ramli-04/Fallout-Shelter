## Information to prepare for a selected asset

- Exact model/sheet filenames, original creator and a small publisher preview link.
- ESSAIM's separate-game context, intended distribution platforms and whether commercial release/public source distribution is intended. Do not invent these project commitments.
- Required modifications: recoloring, resizing, GLB conversion, optimization, sprite rendering and animation retargeting, as applicable.
- Whether original/modified source files will be present in a public repository, or only packaged runtime files.
- Any contributors, textures, fonts, logos or base-game components that the creator cannot license.

## Record a response before import

Keep the exact grant, date, source/contact URL, covered filenames/version, permissions and obligations in an asset-specific notice. Do not publish a private conversation without permission; retain a private record and publish an agreed license notice. Update `catalog.json`, `catalog.csv` and `LICENSES.md` together.

Permission to continue a PZ map/mod is not automatically permission for separate-game commercial distribution or raw-file redistribution. If permission is declined, limited to PZ, missing, or the origin of selected art remains unclear, retain the hold and use a verified alternative.
