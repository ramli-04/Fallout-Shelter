# Seeded city and terrain

The current implementation is the [Phase 4.5 mountain-valley world](PHASE_45_WORLD.md).
That guide covers districts, curved roads, the expanded map, background mountains,
highway, floor and hillside vaults, navigation, camera controls, executed tests,
performance measurements and limitations.

Use Settings ? Apply seed or Random for another deterministic city. Reset with
the same seed reproduces its layout. The Nuclear Impact Controller map-size menu
controls playable dimensions; Valley (3.75) is the new default. Existing capacities,
probabilities and blast damage formulas remain in effect.

Civilians can walk across clear terrain. Streets and paths are convenient visual
approaches rather than mandatory movement channels. Buildings, reservoir and vault
rock shoulders are navigation obstacles; trees are decorative. The hill and forest
exist even when the rumored fifth shelter does not.
