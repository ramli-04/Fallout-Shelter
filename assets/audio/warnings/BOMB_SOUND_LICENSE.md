# Bomb falling sound

`bomb_dropping_cc0.mp3` is the publicly available high-quality MP3 preview of
**Bomb Dropping.wav** by **morganpurkis** (2017).

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/morganpurkis/sounds/398255/
- Audio: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/398/398255_5937039-hq.mp3
- License: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 / public domain dedication
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Retrieved: 2026-10-16

The source page explicitly identifies the asset as CC0. The downloaded preview
is stored unchanged. Godot adjusts playback pitch/duration to fit the eight-second
bomb descent. No explosion recording is included in this whistle.
